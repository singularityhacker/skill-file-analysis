The Three Biggest Deltas
1

Requirements Discovery

The skill-informed model asks what you need. The raw model builds what it guesses.

Cold: Assumed auth, priorities, drag-and-drop, monorepo
Skills: "What does 'complete' mean? Do you need auth? Multi-user?"
2

Principles Over Tools

The raw model says "use Vitest." The skill-informed model says "test behavior at pre-agreed seams, never mock your own modules."

Cold: Listed frameworks and priority order
Skills: Defined philosophy, banned anti-patterns, cited literature
3

The Full Lifecycle

The raw model's lifecycle is scaffold → implement → deploy. The skill-informed model adds everything before and after.

Cold: Nothing before code. Nothing after deploy.
Skills: Grilling, spec, tickets, review, PR structure, canary, retro
